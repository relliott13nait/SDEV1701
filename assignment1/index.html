Welcome to Peony Care & Growing Guide

Peony plants offer big, fluffy, fragrant flowers in a wide range of colors, forms, and sizes. These resilient, long-lived (some to 100 years) perennial bushes have a history of popularity as a garden plant.

Peony Basics

Learn how to plant, care for, and design with peonies for a spectacular garden display.

  • Tree Peonies: Woody shrubs that bloom first in the season.
  • Itoh (Intersectional) Peonies: Hybrid peonies combining the best traits of herbaceous and tree types.

Sunlight & Growing Zones

Here are best places to plant peonies

  • Best suited for USDA Zones 2-8.
  • Require 6 to 8 hours of full sun daily, though tree peonies prefer light shade in extreme heat.
  • Blooming season: April – June, depending on the variety.

Fragrance, Colours & Pest Resistance

  1. Peonies come in white, pink, red, coral, maroon, and yellow. Many varieties change colors as they bloom!
  2. Many peonies have a sweet, citrusy, or spicy scent.
  3. Resistant to deer & rabbits but may attract Japanese beetles.
